# ATDT privacy statement

ATDT is an offline reader for the Phrack archive. This statement is also shown in the app, under Settings → Privacy.

## No personal data

ATDT collects no personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reports or tracking of any kind.

## Network

The app connects to two sites, and only while you download the archive: archives.phrack.org, for the issues, and phrack.org, for their release dates. Requests identify the app ("ATDT/1.0") and carry nothing about you. As with any web request, those sites see your IP address; their own policies apply.

## On your device

The archive, your reading positions, the search index and your settings stay in the app's private storage. They never leave the device and are excluded from cloud backups. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Permissions

Internet access, to download the archive. Nothing else.
